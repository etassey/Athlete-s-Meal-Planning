🏃 Build the Athlete Plate

Quality • Quantity • Timing for an Endurance Athlete

👥 Your Group

📖 The Scenario

You are the nutrition planner for a college endurance runner (70 kg / 154 lb). The athlete will complete a 2-hour run at 7:00 AM.

Your job: build meals for the whole day with the right foods (quality), at the right time (timing), so the athlete gets enough carbohydrates, protein, and healthy fats.
⏱️7:00 AM2-hour run
🍞420–700 gcarbs per day
🍗 84–119 g protein per day

🧭 How To Do This Activity

  1. Read the timing guide below. It tells you what kind of food belongs at each time.
  2. Pick foods from the Food Bank at the top of the page. Colors show the nutrient type: carb, protein, carb + protein, veg/fruit, or fat.
  3. Drag each food into a meal box. On a phone or tablet, tap the food first, then tap the meal box. Tap a food in a meal to remove it.
  4. Read the feedback under each meal. A yellow ⚠️ tells you what is missing. A green ✅ means the meal fits its goal.
  5. Click "Check My Whole Day" at the bottom. When all meals are green, copy your plates onto the whiteboard with pictures or drawings.

Remember: you don't need to calculate grams. Just make sure each meal matches its timing and key nutrients.

⏰ Timing Guide

TimeGoalKey Nutrients
1–2 hrs before exerciseQuick energyMostly carbohydrates
During long exercise (>90 min)Maintain blood glucoseSmall amounts of carbohydrate sources
Within 60 min after exerciseRecoveryCarbohydrate + protein
Rest of dayReplenish energy storesBalanced meals

💡 Tips: Right before the run, choose foods that digest quickly (go easy on fat, protein, and fiber). A balanced plate has a carb, a protein, a fruit or vegetable, and a healthy fat.
